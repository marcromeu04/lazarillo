# Solve a real case from an AI startup

Participant brief for the Karumi × Comillas hackathon at ICAI. Web version: https://karumi.ai/hackathon

## The case

An AI agent has to demo a product it has never seen before. Pick one track, build something real with any AI tool, and defend it in 3 minutes.

- **When:** Wednesday, November 4, 12:30 to 2:00 pm
- **Where:** ICAI, room 308
- **Who:** Teams of 3 to 5, one track each. Any AI tool goes.
- **Prize:** An interview at Karumi for the best teams

## The tracks

Each track is one of the three questions on the poster. Pick one and go deep.

### Track 1: How does it learn to find its way around the app?

An agent lands on an app it has never seen. Build something that helps it understand where it is and how to get anywhere.

Some examples:

- A fast, accurate model that tells which screen it’s on from a screenshot
- A tool that turns a handful of screenshots into a map of the app
- An agent that explores a site on its own and quickly reports what it found

### Track 2: What does it show first?

A visitor has 3 minutes and one thing on their mind. Build something that decides what’s worth showing first, and why.

Some examples:

- Paste a product’s URL and a buyer type, get a 3-minute demo plan
- A ranker that finds the screen most likely to make someone say “wow”
- Three questions to the visitor that pick the path through the demo

### Track 3: How does it know the demo worked?

A demo is more than an agent clicking through an app. Build something that tells whether it worked for the visitor, with evidence, not a feeling.

Some examples:

- A high-quality eval that reads a demo transcript and says whether the visitor got it
- A live detector that spots when the visitor gets lost and tells the agent what to do
- A post-demo check-in people actually want to answer

## Rules

- Teams of 3 to 5, one track each.
- Any AI tool goes.
- If we can’t click it when you present, it doesn’t count. A laptop or a link both work.

## How we judge

1. **It works:** We can click it when you present.
2. **Creativity:** The angle you took on the case.
3. **Shipped with AI:** How much you got done with AI in the time you had.
4. **Real-world value:** Whether it would matter to a real company.

## Your 3 minutes

At the end every team defends what it built in 3 minutes, covering four things:

1. What you built
2. What it proves
3. Its weakest part
4. What you’d add with one more week
